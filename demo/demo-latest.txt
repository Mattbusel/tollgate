=== 9. Cost comparison (all numbers measured on testnet in this run) ===
                                                   txs  gas (Tgas)  tx fees (NEAR)
  1 settlement covering 200 calls                    1       3.228      0.00027813
  200 per-call transfers                           200      89.273      0.00892730
  full channel lifecycle, 220 calls                  5      14.073      0.00122872

  gas: one settlement 3.228 Tgas vs 200 transfers 89.273 Tgas (27.7x).
  gas: whole lifecycle (open, settle, settle2, start_close, finalize) 14.073 Tgas vs 89.273 Tgas (6.3x).
  money: lifecycle tx fees 0.001228 + channel storage 0.002900 = 0.004128 NEAR vs 0.008927 NEAR of transfer fees.
  break-even: storage plus lifecycle fees equal about 93 per-call transfers; past that point each extra call adds no chain cost until the next settlement.
  latency per paid call: 7 ms median with a voucher (including the upstream API) vs 1997 ms median for an on-chain transfer.
